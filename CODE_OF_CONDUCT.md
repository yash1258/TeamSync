# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and discuss ideas, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and product.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or intimidation.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, or contributors that violate this code of conduct.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it through a private channel to project maintainers.
